# Auto Silent Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

Auto Silent Timer is developed and operated by Tim Woodruff.

## Data Collection

Auto Silent Timer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user data. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party data collection software.

## On-Device Data

Auto Silent Timer stores only the following settings locally on the user's device:

1. Whether silent-mode monitoring is enabled.
2. The user's most recently selected timer duration and time unit.
3. The end time of an active silent-mode timer.

This information remains on the device and is used only to provide the app's timer and ringer-restoration features. Uninstalling the app removes this locally stored app data, subject to the device's Android backup and restore settings.

## Device Permissions

Auto Silent Timer requests Android permissions only to provide its core features:

1. Display over other apps: shows the timer prompt after the user switches the phone to silent or vibrate.
2. Do Not Disturb access: detects relevant sound-mode changes and restores the ringer when the timer ends.
3. Notifications: displays the persistent monitoring status and timer completion notices.
4. Exact alarms: restores sound at the time selected by the user, including while the device is idle.
5. Foreground service: continuously monitors user-initiated ringer-mode changes while the feature is enabled.
6. Start after reboot: resumes monitoring and restores any timer that was active before the device restarted.

These permissions do not cause personal information to be transmitted off the device.

## Children

Auto Silent Timer is a general utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
